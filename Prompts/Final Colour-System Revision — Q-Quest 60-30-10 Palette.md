---

# 1. APPLY THE 60/30/10 RULE BY VISUAL DOMINANCE

Do NOT mechanically divide every component into literal percentages.

Use the 60/30/10 rule as a hierarchy of visual dominance.

The palette should feel balanced at every stage.

---

# 2. DARK / NEGATIVE WORLD

The opening Q_QUEST environment should use:

### ~60%
`#050005` / `#470A4D`

### ~30%
`#723576` / `#9855AA`

### ~10%
`#CD8AD0` / `#FEB8FF` / white

This should create:

> deep negative space + purple dimensionality + soft quantum highlights.

Avoid neon saturation.

---

# 3. LIGHT / POSITIVE WORLD

The final Launch Pad world should invert the visual hierarchy:

### ~60%
`#FFF5FF` / `#FFFFFF`

### ~30%
`#9855AA` / `#CD8AD0`

### ~10%
`#470A4D` / `#FEB8FF`

This creates an intentional positive/negative inversion.

---

# 4. THE TRANSITION MUST MORPH BETWEEN THE TWO PALETTE STATES

Do NOT use a generic:

```text
dark purple
→ white
```

crossfade.

The transition should progress through the palette:

```text id="n2rg5n"
#050005
↓
#470A4D
↓
#723576
↓
#9855AA
↓
#CD8AD0
↓
#FEB8FF
↓
#FFF5FF
↓
#FFFADE
↓
#FFFFFF
```

Use a non-linear interpolation curve so the darker tones dominate the early transition and the lighter tones emerge progressively.

The pixel curtain itself should carry this colour transformation.

---

# 5. COLOUR POLARITY

Use the same colours differently in each world.

### Dark world

Light colours = energy/accent.

### Light world

Dark colours = typography/structure.

This creates a visual polarity shift without making the website feel like two unrelated themes.

---

# 6. Q_QUEST

Keep Q_QUEST primarily:

- white
- slightly translucent
- smoked
- subtly dimensional

Use `#FEB8FF` or `#CD8AD0` only as restrained material accents.

Do NOT make it neon pink.

---

# 7. LAUNCH PAD

Use:

> `#470A4D`

as the primary display colour against the light environment.

Supporting typography can use:

- `#723576`
- `#9855AA`

Do not use pure black unless necessary for contrast.

The dark purple should become the main structural contrast in the light world.

---

# 8. CARDS

Keep cards predominantly:

- white
- `#FFF5FF`

Use:

- `#470A4D` for primary text/borders
- `#9855AA` for secondary elements
- `#CD8AD0` for subtle accents
- `#FEB8FF` only for special interaction states

Do NOT turn the cards into purple blocks.

They should remain clean objects inside the environment.

---

# 9. SPHERE MATERIAL

### Main dark sphere

Use:

- core: `#470A4D` / `#723576`
- atmospheric body: `#9855AA`
- Fresnel: `#CD8AD0`
- highlight: `#FEB8FF`

### Final ambient sphere

Use:

- translucent `#9855AA`
- soft `#CD8AD0` edge
- tiny `#FEB8FF` highlight
- white/`#FFF5FF` atmospheric contribution

The final sphere should be subtle and almost blend into the light world.

---

# 10. PIXEL TRANSITION COLOUR BEHAVIOUR

The pixel shader should use the palette as a controlled evolution.

Early:

- #050005
- #470A4D

Middle:

- #723576
- #9855AA

Transition:

- #CD8AD0
- #FEB8FF

Emergence:

- #FFF5FF
- #FFFADE
- #FFFFFF

Do not allow arbitrary shader-generated hues outside the approved palette.

Tiny interpolation variations are acceptable.

# 12. MINIMAL FUTURISM

Do NOT interpret the palette as permission to add more glow.

The design should remain:

- minimal
- clean
- futuristic
- premium
- game-like

Avoid:

- neon cyberpunk
- excessive bloom
- excessive purple
- rainbow gradients
- random accent colours
- overly saturated UI

The palette itself should provide the visual sophistication.

---
